# Primary Sources & Authority Verification Register

**Reference Audit Date:** 23 November 2026  
**Jurisdiction:** India (National BIS/NBC codes + State/UT statutory building rules)

## Source Hierarchy

1. **Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)** — Indian Standards (IS), Special Publications (SP), and National Building Code of India (NBC 2016 / SP 7:2016).
2. **State & UT Statutory Building Rules & Official Gazettes** — Controlling legal authority for setbacks, FSI/FAR, height, parking, and approvals (superseding generic model rules wherever notified).
3. **Government Technical Manuals & Hazard Portals** — BMTPC Vulnerability Atlas, GSI Bhusanket, IMD Climate Normals, CGWB Groundwater Yearbooks, MoEFCC PARIVESH (CRZ), CPWD Specifications/DSR, CPHEEO Manuals, BEE Eco-Niwas Samhita, and NDMA Guidelines.

## Active 2025–2026 Edition-Check Flags

- **SP 7:2016 vs SP 7:2026**: Verify current official BIS transition scope between NBC 2016 (SP 7:2016) and SP 7:2026 catalogue listings.
- **IS 1893 (Part 1):2025 (Seventh Revision)**: Updated earthquake-resistant design criteria; do not rely solely on 2016 textbook values.
- **IS 875 (Part 1):2026**: Supersedes IS 875 (Part 1):1987 for unit weights/dead loads.
- **IS 875 (Part 3):2015 (Amd 2016 & 2020)**: Verify all wind-load amendments.
- **IS 456:2000 (Reviewed 2025)**: Apply all active amendments and linked material/testing standards.
- **IS 1904:2021 & IS 1892:2021**: Current foundation design and subsurface investigation standards.
- **IS 1077:2025 (Sixth Revision)**: Updated common burnt-clay building bricks specification.
- **IS 18168:2023 & IS 16700:2023**: Specialised modern criteria for base-isolated and tall concrete buildings.
- **State Building Rules (2025–2026 Amendments)**: Confirm latest State Gazette amendments before design submission.

## Copyright & Code-Value Policy

Proprietary BIS/NBC numerical tables and licensed clause text are never copied verbatim or fabricated. Where licensed standard tables govern (such as seismic zone factors, concrete exposure cover/grade limits, or State setback tables), chapters state *"Verify in the current licensed standard / project specification"* and link directly to the official BIS or State portal.
